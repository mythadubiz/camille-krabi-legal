2. Description of the App

Camille in Krabi is an interactive bedtime storybook app for children aged 3 and older. The App features:

  • 20 illustrated episodes set in Krabi, Thailand
  • 112 hand-painted illustrations in a warm, hand-drawn anime watercolor style
  • Voice narration in 9 languages (French, English, German, Thai, Spanish, Chinese, Italian, Portuguese, Vietnamese)
  • Voice recording functionality for personalized narration
  • A traditional Thai khlui flute lullaby as background music
  • A poetic moral lesson in each episode
  • Optional iCloud synchronization across devices
